# just_vibes_live.io
Just Vibes Fantasy Football 

## Live Dashboard
- Left sidebar: all 4 matchups with live per-team scores
- Main panel: every rostered player currently in an NFL game, jersey/team-colored, with live fantasy points — toggle between **Starters** and **All rostered**
- Right panel: **Fantasy alerts** feed (touchdowns/sacks/INTs/injuries for your league's players) plus a **Recent plays** ticker across all live games
- Gold touchdown flash and skull/sack overlay animations trigger in real time
- Sticky header (top bar + tabs + live scoreboard strip) stays pinned across every tab

## League Analysis
- **Power Rankings**: expandable team cards (rank, PRS, letter grade, record, PF) — expand for position-by-position grades, this week's Start/Sit swap alerts, starting lineup, and the season-long Season Projected Grade breakdown
- **Trade Recommendations**: complementary surplus/need pairings between teams
- **Waiver Recommendations**: ranked by projected value added and Sleeper add-velocity
- **Trade & Waiver Sandbox**: build a hypothetical move and see the point impact on a cloned roster (doesn't touch real data)
- Sidebar: latest NFL news carousel, injury report, and Luck & Lineup Efficiency list

## Highlights & Recaps
- **Hall of Shame**: auto-generated roast badges each week — Mangenius (bench mismanagement), Fraud Alert (worst score that still won), Unnecessary Roughness (brutal loss margin), Heartbreak Hotel (lost to the week's top scorer)
- **Games to Watch**: Game of the Week (smallest projected spread) and any Trade Revenge Games (matchups against a team you traded with earlier this season)

## Playoff Calculator
- Monte Carlo simulation (2,000 runs) of the remaining schedule using each team's actual scoring distribution
- Ranked playoff odds bar list — seed, record, PF, and live playoff-make percentage, with the "in the field" cutoff highlighted
- Falls back to "regular season complete" messaging once there's nothing left to simulate

## League History
- **Trophies & Shaming Wall**: all-time scoring leaders, win% leaders, championships, "shame" (most last-place finishes), and best win streaks, plus single-game high/low scores
- **Head-to-Head Records**: collapsible owner-by-owner matrix (win-loss record for every pairing, all-time)
- **Historical Draft Performance**: best late-round steal and biggest first-round bust per season
